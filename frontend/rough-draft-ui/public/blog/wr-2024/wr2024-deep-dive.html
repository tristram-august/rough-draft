Reading notes

  • Malik Nabers tore his ACL four games into 2025 — his per-game rate that season was still elite, but the combined totals here reflect roughly 1.25 healthy seasons, not two.
  • Roman Wilson missed all of his 2024 rookie year (0 games) and only debuted in 2025 — his numbers are essentially a single partial season.
  • Combined EPA is total receiving expected-points-added across every regular-season game played in 2024–25 — it rewards value added per target, not just volume, which is why low-target players like Devaughn Vele and high-target players like Xavier Legette can end up on opposite sides of the ranking despite similar pedigree.
  • Production rank and slot delta are computed only among receivers who recorded at least one regular-season target (32 of 35). Tejhaun Palmer, Brenden Rice, and Cornelius Johnson never saw the field and are shown in the reference table but not ranked.
  • Postseason stats are not included. All figures are regular season only.
